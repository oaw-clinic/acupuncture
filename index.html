Yin Yang Philosophy

If you realize Yin (the unmanifest source) and Yang (the physical form), you can understand everything in the universe. Both Yin and Yang are essentially one; we perceive them as two distinct states only to comprehend them fully. Yin and Yang are not separate entities—Yin is unmanifested and subtle, while Yang appears as its outward physical form. No Yang exists without Yin, and Yin without Yang remains unmanifested because it lacks physical expression..

Acupuncture FAQs

Simple answers to common questions.

What is acupuncture?

Acupuncture is an ancient medicine system that works on the energy body. Historical evidences exist dating as early as 2697-2596 B.C.E.

What is Energy body?

Human body has different layers as Physical body, Chemical body and Energy body. Any changes in the energy body reflect in the Chemical body and Physical body, the vice versa also holds true. The physical body is body that we can see with our bare eyes. The Chemical body is also visible based on the chemicals and hormones of the body, it is also subtle as these chemicals and hormones manifest into our mind. Therefore, the chemical body can also be called as the mind body. Energy body is invisible in nature, it is made up of energy flow through the channels and meridians on the surface of the body which are connected with our internal vital organs. This energy has the characteristics of the five elements of the universe namely Fire, Earth, Air, Water and Space(termed as Wood in Acupuncture science).

What is a Disease?

Imbalance of the energy body is considered as the disease, the symptoms manifest in the chemical and physical bodies.

How is a disease diagnosed?

Acupuncture doesn't rely on machines to diagnose human health. There's no need of any tests, scans or x-rays for diagnosing a disease. Acupuncture uses human interactions, observations and pulse diagnosis to identify the imbalanced elements of the energy body.

How is the treatment given?

Using the diagnostic methods, the practitioner identifies the imbalanced elements, the practitioner arrives at an accurate point on the surface of body which can treat the imbalance. The practitioner stimulates the point using a gentle touch or piercing with a fili form needle. Once the energy body is set in balance, the chemical body and physical body also respond to the changes in the energy body. Symptoms begin to subside, morbids are eliminated with ease and optimum health is restored.

How many treatments would be required?

To be honest, definite timelines cannot be given as condition of each body and mind would vary. For acute conditions, cure is potentially felt within 2-4 sittings or earlier. While for chronic conditions, cure can be potentially achieved in 3-6 months or earlier. However, we can guarantee that the curing process would definitely start after taking the first time treatment itself.

What is a sitting?

Sitting is an appointment with your practitioner where the current elemental imbalance is diagnosed and treatment is given. There should be 7 to 10 days between sittings. In some severe cases, additional sitting can be taken after 3 days.

What diseases can be cured by Acupuncture?

Acupuncture has the potential of curing all the diseases. Every disease can be related to the five elemental imbalance. Patience, Faith and perseverance are the key to success.

Are there any food restrictions for Acupuncture?

There are no food restrictions recommended by Acupuncture. You should refrain from bad habits like Smoking, Consuming Alcohol or any kind of intoxications.

Can I take Acupuncture treatment along side my Allopathy medication?

It is strictly advised to refrain from Allopathy medication as they suppress the morbids that Acupuncture tries to eliminate.

Is it really possible to live without medicines in this world?

Absolutely, by creation the human body possesses the inherent capability of curing itself. Acupuncture lifestyle works based on this principle that the human body never makes a mistake and it can cure itself. More than 10000+ AcuHealers and their families in South India are following the Acupuncture lifestyle.

What is Acupuncture lifestyle?

Acupuncture lifestyle is by which one lives by eating only when hungry, drinking water only when thirsty, getting to bed early, rising early and giving rest to the body whenever it demands. For any body ailments, they avoid Allopathic drugs and take Acupuncture treatment. They also keep the mind calm by embracing love and compassion towards others; avoiding resentment and jealousy against others.
